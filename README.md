# Seer Market Observatory

Redesign my Seer six-interface competition entry from scratch with a distinctive creative direction called THE OBSERVATORY.

Do not produce another generic crypto dashboard. Avoid repetitive cards, excessive rounded containers, generic gradients, glowing purple borders, and the standard dark trading-terminal aesthetic.

CORE CONCEPT:
Seer is an instrument for examining possible futures. A question enters the protocol and becomes a price, a distribution, a comparison between possible worlds, a selection forecast, or a risk estimate. Make that transformation the central visual metaphor across the entire ecosystem.

BRAND:
Use the Electric Foundation Figma brand kit as the source of truth. Preserve its recognizable identity, violet and lavender palette, typography direction, and relevant graphic motifs. Make the experience more editorial, experimental, and data-driven without abandoning the brand.

Visual direction:
- Use a sophisticated light editorial canvas with deep ink sections and restrained EF violet accents.
- Build a precise grid with oversized editorial headlines, thin technical rules, compact monospace data labels, and carefully composed charts.
- Use orbital diagrams, contour lines, branching paths, probability distributions, and geometric annotations as functional visual elements.
- Every graphic must explain something about the market, not just decorate the page.
- Keep the design responsive, accessible, and fast.
- Avoid excessive animation. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Build six connected, working routes:

1. HOME: An interactive ecosystem constellation showing Seer at the center and its five applications around it. Each node opens the relevant product. Include a compelling hero, a clear explanation of information finance, and featured markets.

2. APP: A market atlas with powerful search, type filters, compact data rows, probability signatures, liquidity, volume, and meaningful market detail pages. Prioritize exploration over decorative cards.

3. DEEP: A many-outcome possibility field. Show the complete outcome distribution, a ranked candidate list, forecast comparisons, and a detailed view of an illustrative grant-evaluation market. Make uncertainty visible.

4. FUTARCHY: A counterfactual comparison instrument. Put the same success metric on one shared scale for the proposal-passes and proposal-fails scenarios. Show the difference, assumptions, and historical estimates. Markets inform the DAO; they do not make its decision.

5. OPPORTUNITY MARKETS: A candidate field with selection odds, available slots, a probability distribution, and a visual selection frontier. Include a reviewer shortlist interaction. Explicitly preserve human final authority.

6. RISK PRICING: A comparative risk spectrum with protocols on a common scale, uncertainty ranges, risk history, and a comparison table. Clearly distinguish market estimates from verified facts and never imply that risk estimates guarantee safety.

PRODUCT QUALITY:
Use one coherent design system but give each application its own information architecture and data visualization. Include working navigation, functional search and filters, interactive charts where appropriate, and responsive layouts.

Use real public Seer data only when it can be fetched reliably. Otherwise, label all sample data clearly. Do not fabricate live data, performance statistics, or real trades. No blockchain connection is required for the prototype.

Build all six pages, not just the homepage. Make the experience feel art-directed and intentionally designed by a product designer. Prioritize a complete, polished first version that can be published as a public demo.
